At a Glance: The Biometric Rollout in Numbers
The government set out to biometrically enroll all residents by 2023. Mandatory enrollment has since grown from 16 million in late 2024 to over 20 million by mid-2026, yet access to the NID remains restricted to citizens who already hold formal documentation.
