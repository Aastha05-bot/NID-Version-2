Research Areas
The study was carried out between September 2022 and November 2023 across three purposely selected districts — Kathmandu, Sarlahi, and Khotang — supplemented by an additional round of investigative interviews in Kathmandu in November 2024. Khotang and Sarlahi were selected based on the government’s enrollment performance assessment, representing contrasting administrative environments: Khotang was categorized as “successful”, whereas Sarlahi was identified as “difficult”.
